# Ecosystem Builders

This chapter is for **external builders** integrating Cosm into wallets, terminals, launchpads, bots, and vault products.

It is **not** Cosm’s internal engineering handbook. If you are a creator learning how Cosm works, start with [Basic & Mechanism](./basic-and-mechanism/README.md) first — then return here when you are ready to integrate.

## Choose your path

| Role | Start here |
|------|------------|
| Understand the protocol model | [Basic & Mechanism](./basic-and-mechanism/README.md) |
| Index tokens & trade | [Wallet & Terminal & Bot Builders](./wallet-and-terminal-and-bot-developers/README.md) |
| Launch tokens from your app | [Token Launcher Builders](./token-launcher-developers/README.md) |
| Build tax vault strategies | [Vault Builders](./vault-developers/README.md) |
| Triggers & release notes | [Preview](./preview/README.md) |
| Copy/paste addresses | [Deployed Contract Addresses](./deployed-contract-addresses.md) |

## Recommended order

1. Read **Basic & Mechanism** so status, quote, tax, and graduation vocabulary are clear  
2. Open the role chapter that matches your product  
3. Keep Portal as the single trading surface in your UX — before and after DEX graduation
