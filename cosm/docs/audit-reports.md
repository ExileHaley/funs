# Audit Reports

Security reviews for Cosm smart contracts will be published here as they become available.

Until reports are linked, ecosystem builders should:

- Integrate only the canonical proxies listed in [Deployed Contract Addresses](./developers/deployed-contract-addresses.md)
- Treat tax routing, vault strategies, migration, and automated triggers as high-sensitivity surfaces
- Simulate launches and swaps before enabling production traffic
- Disclose fee and tax behavior clearly to end users

If you are conducting a review or need a formal security contact channel, use the contacts listed in [Official Links](./official-links.md) once published.
